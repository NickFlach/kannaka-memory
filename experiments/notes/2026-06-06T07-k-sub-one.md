# K=0.5 sub-threshold sweep — plateau confirmed

**Date:** 2026-06-06T07 UTC
**Branch:** kannaka-curiosity/2026-06-06T07
**Code changes:** None — env-var only test (KURAMOTO_COUPLING=0.5)
**Status:** NULL RESULT — no improvement

---

## Hypothesis

The K-sweep from T00 (PR #142) found a monotone fitness improvement as coupling
strength decreased: K=5.0 (0.226) → K=3.0 (0.177) → K=1.0 (0.138 avg). T00 notes
explicitly flagged "K=1.0 may still be above threshold; K=0.5 or K=0.25 might
perform better or worse."

**Prediction:** K=0.5 continues the monotone trend. Less coupling = more phase
diversity per category = harder adversarial perturbation = higher xi. Expected
fitness below 0.133 (≥0.005 improvement threshold).

---

## Method

Three trials, env-var only: `KURAMOTO_COUPLING=0.5 DRIVE_A=0.1 DRIVE_SCOPE=all`
Default DREAM_MODE (stage_sync). No code changes.

## Analysis

**Trend plateaued at K=1.0.** K=0.5 gives avg fitness 0.138 — indistinguishable
from the K=1.0 baseline. The improvement threshold of ≥0.005 is not met (Δ = +0.000).

### Why the trend stopped

The K-sweep improvement from K=5→1 was driven by reduced over-synchronization:
at K=3.0 the Kuramoto step was forcing phases toward category attractors, compressing
diversity. The T00 notes hypothesized K=1.0 was "below or near the synchronization
threshold." K=0.5 confirms that K=1.0 was already in the sub-threshold regime —
cutting coupling in half makes no further difference to xi (avg 0.852 vs ~0.862 at K=1.0).

### metric shifts vs K=1.0 baseline

- **xi:** roughly equal (0.852 avg vs ~0.862). Trial-to-trial variance is still large (0.772–0.892), driven by the unseeded adversarial RNG in eval_xi_robustness_v2.
- **carrier_emergence:** 0.549 vs 0.568 at K=1.0 — marginal decrease (-0.019). Not enough to affect fitness significantly.
- **magic_R:** dropped from 0.250 (K=1.0) to 0.161 (K=0.5). Stage_sync at even lower coupling is barely organizing phases at all; R reflects near-random phase distribution. This is consistent with K=1.0 being at the synchronization threshold: below it, the Kuramoto step is pure noise.
- **query_gravity:** 0.478 vs 0.469 — essentially unchanged.

### Implication for the K-coupling hypothesis

K=1.0 is the effective lower plateau in fitness space. Reducing coupling further
does not improve xi because the coupling is already too weak to meaningfully alter
phase structure. The optimal K sits at the boundary between "enough coupling to
organize categories" and "not so much that it over-synchronizes." K=1.0 appears to
be right at that boundary.

Testing K=0.25 is likely to show similar or slightly worse results (carrier_e may
degrade further). Not recommended.

## Decision

**No improvement.** K=0.5 avg 0.138 = K=1.0 baseline 0.138. Threshold not met.
No code changes to revert.
