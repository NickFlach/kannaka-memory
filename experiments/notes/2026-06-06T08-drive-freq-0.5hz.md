## Hypothesis

`DRIVE_FREQ_HZ=0.5 Hz` under `DREAM_MODE=interference_relax` will improve fitness
below the current best (interference_relax avg 0.149).

Mechanism: Over a 16-cycle dream chain with `dt_per_cycle=0.125` (t = 0 to 1.875 s),
the drive is `amplitude *= (1 + A × sin(2π × freq × t))`. At 0.5 Hz, this becomes
`sin(π × t)`:
- Cycles 0-8 (t = 0 to 1.0 s): consistently positive, rising to peak +A at cycle 4
- Cycles 9-16 (t = 1.125 to 1.875 s): gently negative, suppressing weakest memories

At default 2.0 Hz, the drive completes 3.75 full oscillations — alternating between
boost and suppression every 0.5 s. The 0.5 Hz "half-cycle arc" provides sustained early
boosting that should amplify carrier structure far more coherently.

**Prediction:** carrier_e rises from 0.497 toward 0.52+; transfer stays ~0.750; possible
fitness avg < 0.144 (threshold for confirmed improvement over prior 0.149 best).

## Analysis

### Why carrier_emergence jumped so dramatically (0.497 → 0.935)

At 2.0 Hz default, the drive oscillates 3.75 times during the chain, alternating
between boost and suppression. Memories experience alternating amplitude changes that
partially cancel — carrier structure is built and broken repeatedly.

At 0.5 Hz, cycles 0-8 provide consistent positive drive peaking at +10% boost
(A=0.1 × sin(π×t)), then cycles 9-16 provide modest suppression (-7% maximum).
The first 8 cycles coherently amplify the bimodal carrier structure before consolidation,
while the second 8 cycles prune weakly-linked memories without disrupting the already-formed
carrier scaffold. This is effectively a "build then refine" arc.

The carrier_emergence jump from 0.497 to 0.935 is the largest single-metric gain seen
in the L5 optimization history. The metric was Nyquist-unblocked (commit cfc87f9) and
is now at near-ceiling.

### Transfer score improvement (0.750 → 0.836)

The sustained early amplification also benefits transfer_score. The consistent early
boosting reinforces the transfer-relevant memory associations before the consolidation
step acts on them. Since carrier_e and transfer both improved, the effect is not
carrier-vs-transfer tradeoff — the 0.5 Hz arc is universally better.

### xi remains stochastic

xi_robustness_v2 range 0.256–0.874 across 3 trials. All other metrics (transfer,
carrier_e, magic_R, query_gravity) are deterministic across trials. The stochasticity
originates entirely in `eval_xi_robustness_v2`'s unseeded adversarial RNG. The mean xi
0.559 is slightly worse than the T00 avg (0.607), but the massive carrier_e and transfer
gains swamp this at current fitness weights.

### Fitness weight arithmetic

Improvement vs T00 interference_relax baseline (0.149):
- carrier_e: (0.935 - 0.497) × 0.10 = −0.044 fitness
- transfer: (0.836 - 0.750) × 0.15 = −0.013 fitness
- xi: (0.559 - 0.607) × 0.15 = +0.007 fitness (slight regression)
- Net predicted: −0.050 improvement ≈ observed 0.149 − 0.099 = 0.050 ✓

The 0.5 Hz result fits the fitness formula cleanly.

### magic_R and query_gravity

magic_R = 0.617 (unchanged from T00; deterministic in interference_relax mode).
query_gravity = 0.363 (unchanged). The drive frequency does not affect phase-order
relationships, only amplitudes. This is expected.

## Implications for future fires

1. **Carrier_e is now the dominant well-controlled metric** (0.935, near ceiling). The
   primary variance in fitness is now xi_robustness_v2 (stochastic, range ~0.25–0.87).
   Seeding `eval_xi_robustness_v2` would make the benchmark far more reproducible.

2. **0.5 Hz + default mode (stage_sync)**: only interference_relax was tested here.
   Does 0.5 Hz also improve carrier_e under default mode? Predicted yes — the mechanism
   is frequency-independent of which consolidation stage runs.

3. **Lower drive frequencies**: 0.5 Hz was the lowest priority candidate from Q6.
   Could 0.25 Hz or even lower be even better? At 0.25 Hz, the drive would be positive
   for the entire 16-cycle chain (t max 1.875 < 2.0 = 1/(0.25/2)). This would be
   monotone positive drive — no late suppression. Might over-amplify without pruning.

4. **transfer_score headroom**: transfer is now 0.836 vs theoretical ceiling 1.0.
   The remaining gap (0.164) is potentially addressable through scope experiments.

5. **xi stabilization**: the mean xi 0.559 is slightly lower than T00's 0.607, and
   xi variance is the dominant fitness noise. A future fire could investigate whether
   seeding the xi adversarial RNG or a K-sweep under 0.5 Hz mode changes xi stability.
