## Hypothesis

At DRIVE_FREQ_HZ=0.25 Hz, the sin argument `2π × 0.25 × t` only reaches 2.945 rad
over the 16-cycle dream window (t = 0 → 1.875 s), which stays entirely below π.
sin(2.945) ≈ +0.20, so the drive factor remains strictly ≥ 1.0 throughout — no
negative suppression phase.

**Prediction:** Without the suppression trough (cycles 9–16 at 0.5 Hz), xi-relevant
memories survive intact, potentially raising xi from the baseline ~0.559 avg. carrier_e
may drop moderately (no "refine" pruning step), but the net fitness effect was expected
to be neutral-to-positive if xi improvement compensated.

## Analysis

### carrier_e collapse (−0.297) — the "refine" phase is essential, not incidental

The 0.5 Hz architecture consists of two phases:
- **Cycles 0–8 (build):** positive drive (peak +A at cycle 4), amplitude coherence grows
- **Cycles 9–16 (refine):** negative drive (trough −A×0.95 at cycle 12), weakest
  carrier associations are pruned below the FFT detection threshold

At 0.25 Hz, the build phase is preserved but reshaped: peak shifts to cycle 8 (t=1.0 s)
instead of cycle 4, and the profile is shallower early on. Critically, **cycles 9–16
no longer suppress** — all 16 cycles accumulate amplitude without pruning.

The FFT-based carrier_emergence metric detects a dominant frequency in the
amplitude time series. The 0.5 Hz suppression trough creates a contrast between the
early-boosted and late-suppressed cycles that makes the carrier frequency visible.
Without that contrast, the amplitude envelope is monotone-rising-then-declining,
which is essentially DC + slow sine — no sharp carrier peak. carrier_e collapses
from 0.935 to 0.638.

**The refine phase is not pruning random memories — it is creating the carrier signal
itself.** The negative suppression at cycles 9–16 is the second half of the carrier
waveform. Removing it (via 0.25 Hz) removes the waveform, not just the pruning.

### xi collapse (−0.370) — xi robustness depends on carrier structure, not its absence

The prediction that xi would rise without suppression was wrong. xi dropped from ~0.559
to 0.189. The likely mechanism: adversarial robustness (xi_v2) is protected by the
carrier structure that stage_interference_relax builds through constructive-pair phase
relaxation. With carrier_e weakened (0.638 vs 0.935), the memory amplitude landscape
is flatter, offering less resistance to adversarial perturbation. The suppression phase
doesn't create adversarial gaps — it strengthens the signal that closes them.

### magic_R and query_gravity are frequency-invariant (confirmed)

Both 0.617 and 0.363, identical to the 0.5 Hz baseline. Consistent with T16's finding
that these are set by the irx phase relaxation dynamics, not drive frequency.

### Fitness arithmetic

Dominant drivers of regression vs 0.099 baseline:
- carrier_e: (0.935 − 0.638) × 0.10 weight = +0.030 fitness cost
- xi: (0.559 − 0.189) × 0.15 weight = +0.056 fitness cost (but xi is noisy; t1 may be
  a low draw; true structural xi degradation is likely ≥0.20 penalty)
- transfer: 0 change
- Expected fitness: 0.099 + 0.030 + some xi cost ≈ 0.13–0.18; observed 0.184 ✓

carrier_e is deterministic under irx — no amount of additional trials will change it.
1 trial is sufficient to falsify.

## Implications

1. **The 0.5 Hz suppression trough is architecturally essential,** not merely a
   by-product. The two-phase pattern (build cycles 0–8, refine/suppress cycles 9–16)
   is what creates the carrier signal that both carrier_emergence and xi_robustness
   depend on. Removing the refine phase collapses both.

2. **DRIVE_FREQ_HZ cannot be lowered below 0.5 Hz** without losing carrier structure.
   The 0.25 Hz trial closes the "lower frequency" axis from T16's open-axes list.

3. **The parameter space at DRIVE_FREQ_HZ=0.5 + irx + A=0.10 appears well-characterized:**
   - Amplitude: A=0.10 is the local optimum (T16: A=0.05 and A=0.15 both regress)
   - Frequency: 0.5 Hz is the local optimum (this fire: 0.25 Hz regresses; higher
     frequencies were tested in earlier fires)
   - relax_steps=16, alpha_base=0.10: confirmed optimal (T23/T24 per T16 notes)
   - All remaining open axes under irx appear to require code-level innovation, not
     env-var tuning.

4. **Next research directions:** The irx env-var space appears exhausted. Code-level
   changes to explore: (a) frequency-dependent alpha_base in stage_interference_relax
   (weaker relaxation when drive is suppressing), (b) asymmetric relax_steps splitting
   the build/refine phases, or (c) a different operating point (stage_sync + K < 0.5
   to get carrier_e toward 0.853 with better xi than current K=0.5 default).
