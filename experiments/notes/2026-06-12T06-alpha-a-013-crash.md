The over-tightened regime onset is between 1.92 and 2.08 at 16 steps — consistent with

steps AFTER convergence; this fire shows it also applies to per-step amplitude once the
total pull threshold is crossed.

### Why alpha=0.13 crashes so severely

At alpha=0.12, the A-phase attractor basins become better defined (tighter, more distinct).
B memories find cleaner basins → transfer improves. At alpha=0.13, per-step pull of 0.13
is large enough that within 16 steps the system OVERSHOOTS attractor minima: phases
converge to wrong local minima, or multiple attractors collapse into one. Either way,
the A-landscape loses its basin structure, and B memory integration fails catastrophically.

The 16-step boundary for overshoot is lower than the 20-step boundary (T13) because
each step at 0.13 is more aggressive. The "safe window" for engine_a is:
- Floor: alpha must be ≥0.10 to improve transfer at all (T17 showed 0.08 regresses)
- Ceiling: alpha must be ≤0.12 (0.13 crashes)
- Exact safe range: [0.12, 0.12] — 0.12 is uniquely optimal

### alpha=0.125 not worth testing

The transition at 16 steps occurs somewhere in (0.12, 0.13). A probe at 0.125 might
find a small safe improvement or hit the crash boundary. However:
1. Even if 0.125 is safe and improves transfer to 0.966, Δfitness ≈ −0.0003 — sub-threshold
2. The crash at 0.13 was severe (not a gradual slope), suggesting a sharp transition;
   0.125 is risky and the expected gain is minimal
3. The current threshold (0.007627 − 0.005 = 0.002627) cannot be reached via transfer
   alone even at the fp ceiling
