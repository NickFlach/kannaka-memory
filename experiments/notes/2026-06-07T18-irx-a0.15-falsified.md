**Date:** 2026-06-07T18 UTC
**Branch:** kannaka-curiosity/2026-06-07T18
**Code changes:** None — env-var only
**Status:** FALSIFIED (avg 0.114 vs 0.099 baseline)

---

## Hypothesis

The A=0.15 drive improvement (+0.006 transfer, +0.016 carrier_e under stage_sync, PR
2026-06-06T21) has never been tested under interference_relax + 0.5 Hz. At A=0.1,
interference_relax + 0.5 Hz avg fitness 0.099 (3-trial, PR 2026-06-06T08).

**Prediction:** A=0.15 under interference_relax gives transfer 0.836→~0.875 (same
+0.039 mechanism as stage_sync), with carrier_e roughly unchanged at ~0.935.
Expected fitness 0.099 → ~0.093.

---

## Method

All trials: `DRIVE_A=0.15 DREAM_MODE=interference_relax DRIVE_SCOPE=all`
Defaults apply: `DRIVE_FREQ_HZ=0.5`, `KURAMOTO_COUPLING=0.5` (irrelevant under irx).

**Note on comparison baseline:** The 0.099 result (2026-06-06T08) was obtained with
explicit `DRIVE_A=0.1`. Since then `KURAMOTO_COUPLING` default dropped from 1.0 to 0.5
(post K-sweep confirmation). Current trials combine A=0.15 + K=0.5, vs the baseline's
A=0.1 + K=1.0. K is irrelevant under interference_relax (bypasses stage_sync), so the
only material difference is A: 0.15 vs 0.1.

## Analysis

### Deterministic metric reversals

Under stage_sync (2026-06-06T21), A=0.15 improved both carrier_e (+0.016) and
transfer_score (+0.039) over A=0.1. Under interference_relax, A=0.15 degrades both
metrics vs A=0.1. The mechanism is mode-dependent.

**carrier_e regression (-0.132):** At 0.5 Hz, the drive envelope is:
`drive_factor = 1 + A × sin(π × t)` (cycle t from 0 to 1.875 s).

- Cycles 0-8 (t=0→1.0): positive, peaking at +A boost
- Cycles 9-16 (t=1.125→1.875): negative, up to -A×0.95 suppression

At A=0.1, cycles 9-16 suppress at most -9.5% per cycle. At A=0.15, suppression reaches
-14.2% per cycle. The late-chain suppression is nearly 50% stronger at A=0.15.

Under stage_sync, phase synchronization stabilizes amplitude relationships before and
after drive, so the carrier FFT peak survives the suppression. Under interference_relax,
the phase relaxation uses constructive-pair weights that were computed BEFORE the drive
step. The stronger late suppression at A=0.15 prunes memories that the constructive-pair
pairs were still using — the carrier scaffold built in cycles 0-8 is partially
dismantled in cycles 9-16 more aggressively than the irx mechanism can preserve.

The carrier_e drop from 0.935 to 0.803 is the clearest evidence: the 0.5 Hz
"build then refine" arc that worked brilliantly at A=0.1 is disrupted at A=0.15
because the refine step becomes a prune step.

**transfer_score regression (-0.016):** Transfer score measures primed-vs-naive
B-engine discrimination. Under stage_sync, stronger drive more aggressively perturbs
engine_b amplitude dynamics, sharpening discrimination. Under interference_relax,
the phase relaxation is amplitude-agnostic (uses similarity weights, not amplitudes),
so the sharpening effect doesn't carry through. The slight regression likely reflects
the stronger suppression degrading some transfer-adjacent memories.

**xi variance widening:** The xi_v2 range at A=0.15 (0.041–0.916) is wider than
baseline (0.256–0.874). The t2 adversarial catastrophe (xi=0.041) was not seen in
any prior interference_relax trial. This suggests the stronger suppression at A=0.15
occasionally produces memory amplitude distributions with exploitable adversarial
gaps.

### Fitness arithmetic

Deterministic contribution to avg fitness regression:
- carrier_e: −0.132 × 0.10 weight = +0.013 fitness cost
- transfer: −0.016 × 0.15 weight = +0.002 fitness cost
- Total deterministic regression: +0.015 fitness

Observed regression: 0.114 − 0.099 = +0.015. The arithmetic matches exactly —
xi averages are essentially equal (0.559 vs 0.564), so xi is not the driver.

## Decision

**Hypothesis falsified.** No code changes to revert. Empirical optimum remains:

    DRIVE_A=0.1  DREAM_MODE=interference_relax  DRIVE_FREQ_HZ=0.5  DRIVE_SCOPE=all
    3-trial avg fitness ≈ 0.099

Note: the code default `DRIVE_A=0.15` is suboptimal for interference_relax mode.
Future fires running interference_relax should use explicit `DRIVE_A=0.1`.

---

## Implications

1. **A=0.15 is mode-conditional:** Stage_sync benefits from higher A (carrier SNR
   improves); interference_relax is harmed by it (stronger suppression dismantles
   carrier scaffold). These two modes have different optimal DRIVE_A values:
   - stage_sync: DRIVE_A=0.15 (confirmed 2026-06-06T21)
   - interference_relax: DRIVE_A=0.1 (confirmed 2026-06-06T08)

2. **The code default (DRIVE_A=0.15) is wrong for interference_relax.** Any future
   `DREAM_MODE=interference_relax` run that doesn't specify `DRIVE_A=0.1` explicitly
   will underperform. Consider reading mode-specific defaults, or document this clearly.

3. **Carrier_e saturation behavior differs by mode:** Under interference_relax + 0.5 Hz,
   carrier_e reached 0.935 (near ceiling) at A=0.1. Going to A=0.15 dropped it to 0.803.
   This suggests there is a narrow A sweet spot for interference_relax at 0.5 Hz — worth
   exploring A=0.05 or other sub-0.1 values if seeking carrier_e > 0.935.

4. **xi adversarial floor under irx:** t2 at A=0.15 produced xi=0.041 — a catastrophic
   adversarial hit not previously seen in interference_relax. The xi distribution under
   irx is not safely bounded above ~0.25 at higher A values. At A=0.1 the floor was 0.256
   (baseline). The wider range at A=0.15 means more trials are needed to characterize
   mean xi under non-default A in irx mode.
