# K=0.5: Kuramoto coupling below K=1.0 optimum

**Date:** 2026-06-06T11 UTC  
**Branch:** kannaka-curiosity/2026-06-06T11  
**Code changes:** None — env-var only (KURAMOTO_COUPLING=0.5)  
**Status:** NOT AN IMPROVEMENT — K=1.0 remains empirical optimum

---

## Hypothesis

The K=1.0 confirmatory fire (2026-06-06T00) established K=1.0 as the new optimum
(avg fitness 0.138 vs baseline ~0.18). Its notes flagged: "K=0.5 or K=0.25 might
perform better or worse — K=1.0 may still be above the synchronization threshold."

Prediction: K=0.5 further reduces Kuramoto coupling, leaving phases even more
diverse within categories → xi_robustness_v2 stays high or improves → fitness
improves below 0.138.

Mechanism: K=1.0 was described as a "mixing perturbation rather than a convergence
engine." K=0.5 should amplify this effect.

---

## Setup

All trials: `DRIVE_A=0.1 DRIVE_TOP_FRAC=1.0 DRIVE_SCOPE=all KURAMOTO_COUPLING=0.5`

## Analysis

**K=0.5 is not an improvement.** Three-trial avg 0.160 > K=1.0 avg 0.138.

### xi variance is wider at K=0.5

| K | xi trial 1 | xi trial 2 | xi trial 3 | xi mean | xi std (approx) |
|---|-----------|-----------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| 1.0 | 0.814 | 0.862 | 0.917 | 0.864 | 0.052 |
| 0.5 | 0.898 | 0.524 | 0.642 | 0.687 | 0.196 |

K=1.0 has both *higher mean xi* and *lower variance*. This is the opposite of the
prediction. K=0.5 delivers occasional great xi (trial 1: 0.898) but more often
moderate xi (0.524–0.642), resulting in a worse average.

### Deterministic metrics at K=0.5 vs K=1.0

| metric | K=1.0 (confirmed) | K=0.5 |
|--------|------------------|--------|
| carrier_emergence | 0.5684 | 0.5489 |
| magic_proxy_phase_R | 0.2498 | 0.1432 |
| query_gravity | 0.4691 | 0.4771 |

carrier_e is slightly lower (−0.020), R is much lower (−0.107). query_gravity is
slightly higher (+0.008) at K=0.5. No significant deterministic metric advantage.

### Mechanism revision

The K=1.0 notes proposed: "K=1.0 sits below the synchronization threshold, so
Kuramoto dynamics act as a mixing perturbation." K=0.5 reveals this model is
incomplete. At K=0.5, the coupling is so weak that the dream's phase updates are
nearly noise — xi becomes a lottery rather than a structured outcome. K=1.0
appears to be a stable operating point near (but below) a coupling sweet spot
where the coupling provides just enough structured nudging to consistently
build non-commutative phase arrangements.

The R drop (0.250 → 0.143) at K=0.5 is consistent with less phase coherence
overall — the order parameter falls because phases drift without sufficient
coupling to form correlated clusters. Less coherence isn't inherently bad for xi,
but without structured cluster formation, the adversarial perturbation in
eval_xi_robustness_v2 becomes more effective on average.

---

## Decision

**Not an improvement.** No code changes to revert.

K=1.0 (fitness avg 0.138) remains the empirical optimum under stage_sync.
K=0.5 produces larger xi variance and worse mean fitness (0.160).

---

## Next questions

1. **K=0.25 or K=0**: probably even worse (deeper into the noise regime). The
   data suggests K=1.0 is a local optimum, not just one end of a monotone trend.

2. **Seeding xi evaluation**: the xi stochasticity (SD ~0.05 at K=1.0, ~0.20 at
   K=0.5) is the dominant variance source. A seeded adversarial RNG would reduce
   required trial counts from 3 to 1 for any new config. This is an engineering
   question worth raising — though changing eval_xi_robustness_v2 requires careful
   validation that the score distribution remains the same in expectation.

3. **K=1.0 + DRIVE_FREQ_HZ**: the only clean env-var axis not yet tested at the
   new K=1.0 default. Low expected impact since the drive directly imprints the
   signal at any in-band frequency.

4. **K=1.0 + interference_relax**: K affects only stage_sync; interference_relax
   replaces it. These are mutually exclusive, so their benefits cannot compound.
   The two optimums (K=1.0: 0.138, interference_relax: 0.149) are separate paths,
   and K=1.0 is currently better.
