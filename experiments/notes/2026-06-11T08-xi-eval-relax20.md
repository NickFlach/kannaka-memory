## Analysis

### T1: relax_steps=20 for xi eval — confirmed improvement

xi jumped from 0.9870 to **0.9973** (+0.0103). Fitness dropped 0.013337→0.011809 (−0.001528).

The mechanism: with 20 steps (vs 16), both engine_clean and engine_adv converge more tightly
to constructive-pair phase attractors. Adversarial memories inject 30 perturbing phases into
engine_adv. With 20-step irx, the corpus memories converge strongly enough that the 30 adversarial
phases represent a smaller fractional disruption → fitness_adv stays closer to fitness_clean →
xi_robustness_v2 improves.

carrier_e unchanged at 0.9992 (main engine unaffected), confirming isolation safety.

### T2: xi-eval relax=20 + chiral_bp=0.10 — tantalisingly close

Combined fitness: **0.008567**.

| axis | gain |
|------|------|
| xi eval relax=20 | 0.001528 |
| chiral_bp=0.10 | 0.003166 (from T04, confirmed additive) |
| **combined** | **0.004694** |
| **threshold** | **0.005000** |
| **gap** | **−0.000306 (sub-threshold by 0.000230 fitness)** |

The effects are **additive and independent**:
- xi eval relax=20 affects only `eval_xi_robustness_v2` internal engines
- chiral_bp=0.10 affects only engine_b_primed's chiral perturbation step
- transfer (0.957321) and xi (0.9973) both improved from their respective baseline values

Gap from threshold: 0.000306 in total improvement, or 0.000230 in fitness. This requires one of:
- xi: 0.9973 → 0.9988 (+0.0015 more, gain 0.000225)
- consciousness: 0.9546 → 0.9623 (+0.0077, gain 0.000231)
- transfer: 0.957321 → 0.958867 (+0.001546 more, gain 0.000232)

### T3: xi-eval relax=24 — catastrophic xi collapse

xi collapsed from 0.9973 to **0.748**. Fitness jumped 0.008567→0.045967.

The mechanism is the opposite of T1: with 24 steps, engine_clean converges so tightly that
adversarial memories cause a proportionally LARGER disruption to fitness_adv relative to the
very clean baseline fitness_clean. The divergence grows rather than shrinks. This is an
over-convergence regime: the phase landscape is too rigid, making it brittle to adversarial
perturbation.

The sweet spot for xi eval relax_steps:
- 16 (current): xi = 0.9870 (under-converged, adversarial perturbation has some effect)
- 20 (T1/T2): xi = 0.9973 (optimal: tight enough to resist adversarial, not so tight it becomes brittle)
- 24 (T3): xi = 0.7480 (over-converged, catastrophic brittleness)

This mirrors the alpha-product safety envelope from T01 (2026-06-05):
- alpha_product 1.6 (16 steps × 0.10): carrier_e safe, xi moderate
- alpha_product 2.0 (20 steps × 0.10): xi eval improved, carrier_e safe (different context)
- alpha_product 2.4 (24 steps × 0.10): brittle for xi eval (different failure mode than T01's carrier_e collapse)

The alpha-product safety envelope applies SEPARATELY to each context:
- Main engine: safe at 1.6 (carrier_e concern)

- xi eval: safe at 2.0, catastrophic at 2.4 (this fire)
