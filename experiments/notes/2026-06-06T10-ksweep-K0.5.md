# K=0.5 Kuramoto coupling sweep — sub-K=1.0 exploration

**Date:** 2026-06-06T10 UTC  
**Branch:** kannaka-curiosity/2026-06-06T10  
**Status:** HYPOTHESIS NOT CONFIRMED — no code changes

---

## Hypothesis

K=1.0 is the confirmed L5 default (PR #142 / 2026-06-06T00). The K=3→1 gradient
was monotone-improving: lower coupling → more phase diversity within categories →
higher xi_robustness_v2 → lower fitness. The T00 notes flagged "K=0.5 or K=0.25
might perform better or worse" as an explicit open question.

**Prediction:** K=0.5 extends the gradient. Phase diversity increases further
(xi approaching ceiling near 1.0), transfer_score and carrier_emergence stay
stable, and 3-trial avg fitness drops below K=1.0's confirmed 0.138.

No code change required — env-var `KURAMOTO_COUPLING=0.5` overrides the default.

---

## Method

`KURAMOTO_COUPLING=0.5 DRIVE_A=0.1 DRIVE_SCOPE=all DREAM_MODE=<unset>`

3 trials. Reference: K=1.0 3-trial avg fitness 0.138, xi avg ~0.86.

## Analysis

**Hypothesis falsified.** 3-trial avg fitness 0.145 vs K=1.0's 0.138 — worse by 0.007,
no improvement within the 0.005 threshold.

**The failure mode is bimodal.** Trials t1 and t3 show xi ≈ 0.90–0.97 and fitness
≈ 0.118–0.129 — which would be a new optimum if reliable. Trial t2 has xi collapse
to 0.548 (vs t1/t3's ~0.94 avg), pushing fitness to 0.190. At K=1.0, all three
historical trials had xi ≥ 0.813 — no collapses.

**Interpretation:** K=1.0 sits just above the Kuramoto synchronization threshold
for this corpus. Each dream cycle reliably nudges phases into enough coherence to
defend against the adversarial xi protocol. K=0.5 is below or near the threshold
in a stochastic regime — on most cycles the sync converges (high xi), but
occasionally it doesn't (xi collapses). The carrier_emergence and transfer_score
are deterministic across trials (0.5489 and 0.6805 in successful trials), confirming
the stochasticity is entirely in the xi measurement / sync outcome.

**magic_R comparison:**
- K=1.0: R ≈ 0.250 (T00 notes)
- K=0.5 (high-xi trials): R = 0.1432
- K=0.5 (low-xi trial): R = 0.1971

R continues decreasing below K=1.0, as expected if lower coupling = more phase
entropy. The slight R elevation in the t2 failure trial may reflect partially
ordered phases that look "non-Clifford" by the order-parameter criterion while
being more classically accessible.

**query_gravity** is stable across trials (0.477–0.479) and marginally higher than
K=1.0's 0.469 — the attention-gravity effect is slightly stronger at K=0.5, but
the difference is within noise.

---

## Decision

K=0.5 does not improve over K=1.0. The reliable operating point is K=1.0 where the
phase-diversity mechanism works consistently. Going lower introduces a reliability
failure mode that offsets the upside. K=1.0 remains the confirmed optimum.

## Next fire directions

1. **Is the xi stochasticity seeded?** The xi variance is the dominant noise
   source for all modes (stage_sync and interference_relax). Seeding
   `eval_xi_robustness_v2` would make results more reproducible and reduce
   the 3-trial requirement. Worth checking the eval code.

2. **interference_relax at K=0.5**: K doesn't affect interference_relax (which
   bypasses stage_sync entirely), but does varying K before or after
   interference_relax affect the pre-sync state that interference_relax relaxes?
   Likely not, but untested.

3. **Can the bimodal xi failure mode be characterized?** Trial t2's xi=0.548 at
   K=0.5 means the adversarial attack succeeded. Is this a specific adversary
   configuration, or sampling noise in the eval? One way to probe: run the same
   trial 5 times at K=0.5 and record the xi distribution shape.
