### Why A=0.15 hurts irx+0.5Hz carrier_e

The 0.5 Hz "build then refine" arc: cycles 0-8 provide positive drive (peak +A at t=1.0s), cycles 9-16 provide mild suppression (maximum −A at t=2.0s, but chain ends at t=1.875s). At A=0.10, the suppression phase peaks at −10%; at A=0.15, it peaks at −15%.

Under irx, the phase relaxation step acts on the amplitude-modified memory landscape. At A=0.15, the suppression phase is 50% stronger, over-pruning the carrier amplitude structure that the irx mechanism built during cycles 0-8. The FFT peak at 0.5 Hz drops from 0.935 to 0.803 because the suppression phase now erodes the amplitude bimodality the carrier metric measures.

This is distinct from stage_sync behavior: under stage_sync+0.5Hz, A=0.15 raised carrier_e (0.568 → 0.584) because stage_sync's Kuramoto step is amplitude-neutral and the additional drive amplitude isn't over-cancelled by suppression. Under irx, the phase relaxation interacts with amplitude in a way that makes the suppression phase more destructive.

**The irx optimum is A=0.1. A=0.15 is confirmed bad for irx+0.5Hz.**

## Serendipitous finding — production defaults

With 2 remaining trial runs, tested the current production defaults as a unit:

`DRIVE_A=0.15 DRIVE_FREQ_HZ=0.5 KURAMOTO_COUPLING=0.5 DREAM_MODE=<unset> DRIVE_SCOPE=all`
(all env vars omitted — code defaults)

| trial | fitness | transfer_score | carrier_emergence | xi_robustness_v2 | magic_R | query_gravity |
|-------|---------|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|---------|---------------|
| t1    | 0.118   | 0.695          | 0.853             | 0.773           | 0.140   | 0.457         |
| t2    | 0.116   | 0.581          | 0.853             | 0.863           | 0.222   | 0.426         |
| **2T avg** | **0.117** | **0.638**  | **0.853**         | **0.818**       | **0.181** | **0.442** |

**Prior stage_sync benchmarks:**

| config | fitness avg | carrier_e | xi avg | transfer avg |
|--------|------------|-----------|--------|-------------|
| K=1.0, A=0.1, FREQ=2Hz (old baseline) | 0.138 | 0.568 | 0.864 | 0.654 |
| K=1.0, A=0.15, FREQ=0.5 (T21) | 0.132 | 0.584 | 0.844 | 0.694 |
| K=0.5, A=0.1, FREQ=0.5 (T14) | 0.134 | 0.549 | 0.852 | 0.666 |
| **K=0.5, A=0.15, FREQ=0.5 (this fire, 2T)** | **0.117** | **0.853** | **0.818** | **0.638** |

**carrier_e=0.853 is the highest ever seen under stage_sync** (previous best: 0.584 at T21).

The K=0.5 × A=0.15 × FREQ=0.5 interaction produces a non-linear carrier_e jump. Individual effects:
- K=1.0→K=0.5 at A=0.1+FREQ=0.5: carrier_e 0.568 → 0.549 (slight drop)
- K=1.0+A=0.1→A=0.15 at FREQ=0.5: carrier_e 0.568 → 0.584 (modest gain)
- K=0.5+A=0.15+FREQ=0.5 together: carrier_e **0.853** — synergistic

The mechanism: K=0.5 (weaker Kuramoto sync) reduces amplitude damping, so the stronger A=0.15 drive at FREQ=0.5's coherent arc encounters less synchronization resistance. The carrier amplitude scaffold survives stage_sync at K=0.5 with A=0.15 because the sync step is too weak to average it out. Under K=1.0, the Kuramoto sync is strong enough to partially revert the amplitude structure, limiting carrier_e.

transfer_score is stochastic across these two trials (0.695 vs 0.581) — unusual for this metric. This may indicate K=0.5 introduces stochasticity into the A→B encoding pathway, similar to the bimodal failure mode observed in T10's K=0.5 trials (one trial had xi collapse).

**2 trials is below the 3-trial confirmation threshold.** This is an unconfirmed promising direction, not a proven improvement. The 2T avg of 0.117 is well above the irx+A=0.1 confirmed best of 0.099.

## Decision

**No code changes.** The production defaults (K=0.5, A=0.15, FREQ=0.5, stage_sync) are already in the code. No reversion needed for the irx+A=0.15 trials (env-var only).

**True current optimum remains:** irx + A=0.1 + FREQ=0.5, 3-trial avg fitness **0.099** (T08, 2026-06-06T08). Requires explicit `DRIVE_A=0.1` since code default is now 0.15.

**Production stage_sync defaults give ~0.117 (2T)** — a new stage_sync benchmark substantially better than any previously characterized stage_sync config.

## Implications for next fires

1. **irx+A=0.1 is the confirmed optimum; irx+A=0.15 is firmly closed.** The A=0.1 override must be explicit since the code default changed to 0.15.

2. **Production defaults need 3-trial confirmation.** `cargo run --release --quiet --bin research -- --level 5` (no env vars) should be confirmed in 3 trials. 2T avg 0.117 is promising but insufficient. A confirmed 0.117 avg would establish a new stage_sync baseline; further improvements from here would need to be compared to 0.117.

3. **transfer_score stochasticity at K=0.5+stage_sync.** Under K=0.5, transfer varied 0.581–0.695 across two trials. This warrants attention — it may mean K=0.5 sits in a stochastic transfer regime similar to the xi bimodality seen in T10.

4. **The K×A×FREQ synergy for carrier_e under stage_sync is unexpectedly large.** carrier_e=0.853 at K=0.5+A=0.15+FREQ=0.5 vs 0.584 at K=1.0+A=0.15+FREQ=0.5. Future experiments that seek to push carrier_e further under stage_sync should start from the K=0.5+FREQ=0.5 baseline.

5. **irx vs stage_sync gap remains.** Best confirmed irx (0.099) still beats the best stage_sync (0.117 unconfirmed). The gap is 0.018 in favor of irx. Since irx requires DRIVE_A=0.1 (not the default), a confirmed production-defaults run at 0.117 would narrow that operationally usable gap.
