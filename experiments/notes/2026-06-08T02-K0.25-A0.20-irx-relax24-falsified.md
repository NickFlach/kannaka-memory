## Background

Current empirical optima:
- **stage_sync** (code defaults): `KURAMOTO_COUPLING=0.5 DRIVE_A=0.15 DRIVE_FREQ_HZ=0.5 DRIVE_SCOPE=all` → 3-trial avg fitness **0.104**, carrier_e=0.853, xi=0.873 avg
- **interference_relax**: `DRIVE_A=0.1 DREAM_MODE=interference_relax DRIVE_SCOPE=all` → 3-trial avg fitness **0.099**, carrier_e=0.935, xi=0.559 avg

Three research directions were untested:
1. K=0.25 under stage_sync+A=0.15 — the K×A carrier synergy was discovered at K=0.5; Q: does it extend lower?
2. A=0.20 under stage_sync+K=0.5 — T11 notes explicitly flagged this as "worth 1 trial"
3. irx + relax_steps=24 — Q3 from research questions (relax_steps was 8, now 16 post prior fire; Q: does 24 further help xi?)

## Hypothesis 1: K=0.25 + A=0.15 + stage_sync

**Prediction:** The carrier_e jump from K=1.0 (0.584) to K=0.5 (0.853) reflects increasing drive-frequency transparency as K decreases. K=0.25 extends this trend, pushing carrier_e above 0.90 while xi remains near 0.873 (A=0.15 stabilized xi at K=0.5).

**Mechanism assumption:** Lower K = weaker Kuramoto nudge = more phase diversity = drive frequency more cleanly stamped into amplitude time series.

### Results (1 trial)

| metric | K=0.5+A=0.15 baseline | K=0.25+A=0.15 | delta |
|--------|----------------------|----------------|-------|
| fitness | 0.104 avg | **0.159** | **+0.055 regression** |
| transfer_score | 0.655 avg | **0.462** | **−0.193** |
| carrier_emergence | 0.853 | **0.848** | −0.005 (flat) |
| xi_robustness_v2 | 0.873 avg | 0.739 | −0.134 |
| magic_R | 0.161 | 0.174 | +0.013 |
| query_gravity | 0.446 | 0.445 | ≈0 |

**Hypothesis falsified.** carrier_e did NOT improve — it was flat (0.848 ≈ 0.853). The mechanism assumption was wrong. The carrier_e synergy at K=0.5 is not a monotone function of decreasing K; it is specific to the K=0.5 operating point.

The catastrophic collapse in transfer_score (0.655 → 0.462) reveals the mechanism: Kuramoto coupling creates the amplitude discrimination structure that engine_b uses for transfer. At K=0.5, coupling is just sufficient to establish this structure while remaining transparent to the drive. At K=0.25, coupling is too weak — the B-engine amplitude landscape becomes underdetermined and transfer collapses. K=0.5 is a Goldilocks point, not a direction to continue.

xi also degraded (0.873 → 0.739), consistent with weaker category organization making adversarial attacks easier.

## Hypothesis 2: A=0.20 + K=0.5 + stage_sync

**Prediction (from T11 notes):** The K×A synergy might extend to A=0.20. Under K=0.5, the drive-carrier emergence might be more tolerant of higher amplitude since K=0.5 doesn't fight back against the carrier structure.

### Results (1 trial)

|--------|----------------------|--------------|-------|
| fitness | 0.104 avg | **0.168** | **+0.064 regression** |
| transfer_score | 0.655 avg | **0.384** | **−0.271** |
| carrier_emergence | 0.853 | **0.653** | **−0.200** |

| magic_R | 0.161 | 0.116 | −0.045 |
| query_gravity | 0.446 | 0.459 | +0.013 |

**Hypothesis falsified.** A=0.20 caused both carrier_e (−0.200) and transfer (−0.271) to collapse. xi was nearly unchanged (0.873 → 0.866), confirming xi stability at K=0.5 persists at higher A. But the carrier and transfer mechanisms are both disrupted.

The A=0.20 failure at K=0.5 mirrors the pattern seen at K=1.0 (Q5 fire: carrier_e=0.433, xi=0.444 at K=1.0+A=0.20). The carrier_e collapse at over-drive is mode-independent. The negative-arc cycles (9–16 at 0.5 Hz) produce ~19% suppression at A=0.20, destroying the carrier scaffold regardless of Kuramoto coupling strength. A=0.15 is the hard ceiling for the current drive architecture.

## Hypothesis 3: irx + relax_steps=24

**Prediction:** xi under irx (avg 0.559) is limited by insufficient phase convergence. At relax_steps=16 (doubled from original 8), convergence improves per step. At 24, further convergence → more distinct phase neighborhoods → harder adversarial attacks → xi rises toward 0.7+.

**Code change:** `relax_steps: usize = 16` → `relax_steps: usize = 24` in `stage_interference_relax`.

### Results (1 trial)

| metric | irx+A=0.1 baseline (3-trial avg) | irx+A=0.1 relax24 | delta |
|--------|-----------------------------------|--------------------|-------|
| fitness | 0.099 | **0.243** | **+0.144 regression** |
| transfer_score | 0.836 | 0.672 | −0.164 |
| carrier_emergence | 0.935 | **0.000** | **−0.935 CATASTROPHE** |
| xi_robustness_v2 | 0.559 avg | 0.434 | −0.125 |
| magic_R | 0.617 | 0.657 | +0.040 |
| query_gravity | 0.363 | 0.398 | +0.035 |

**Hypothesis falsified with catastrophic carrier_e collapse.** carrier_e hit 0.000 — the 2 Hz FFT peak was completely eliminated.

**Mechanism:** The irx mode uses the DRIVE step (which builds carrier structure in amplitude time series) BEFORE the phase relaxation step. At relax_steps=16, the phase relaxation is sufficiently limited that the carrier amplitude signature survives. At relax_steps=24, the 8 additional phase relaxation steps (each moving phases toward constructive-pair means) reorganize the amplitude envelope enough to destroy the carrier frequency peak. The amplitude modulation pattern that the drive stamped into the dream chain is smeared out by over-relaxation.

This reveals an important constraint: **irx relax_steps=16 is already at the carrier_e ceiling**. Any further relaxation destroys carrier structure rather than improving xi. The two mechanisms (drive→carrier building, irx→phase convergence) are in direct tension at relax_steps>16.

The code change was reverted. `relax_steps` is restored to 16.

### What remains open

3. **Structural consolidation changes**: stage_boost_prune thresholds or stage_hallucinate parameters — unexplored and outside this fire's scope.

4. **Combining modes**: No mechanism exists to run both stage_sync and irx. But the metrics trade-off is stark: irx wins on transfer+carrier+minor metrics, stage_sync wins on xi. A hybrid would theoretically combine the best of both.
